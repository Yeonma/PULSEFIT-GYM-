OUR WORKOUTS

Strength Training

Build muscle and strength with structured weight training, ready for whatever life lifts your way!

About: Build strength and muscle using squats, presses, rows, and other free-weight or machine movements.

Benefits: Supports muscular strength, bone health, and everyday tasks, from carrying sacks to climbing stairs.

Good for: Beginners and experienced members. Start with manageable weights and focus on proper form.

Example session: Warm-up, squats, chest press, seated rows, shoulder press, and a cool-down.

Cardio

Build your stamina so you do not run out of breath, whether at work, at school, or on the court.

About: Raise your heart rate through brisk walking, cycling, jogging, rowing, or the elliptical machine.

Benefits: Helps develop stamina and supports heart and lung fitness.

Good for: Anyone who wants more endurance or a more active routine. Adjust the pace to your fitness level.

Example session: 5-minute easy warm-up, 15–25 minutes at a comfortable pace, then a cool-down.

Boxing

Sharpen your speed, coordination, stamina, and confidence. Fight for a stronger you!

About: Practice footwork, shadowboxing, bag work, and basic combinations in a safe, guided setting.

Benefits: Develops coordination, conditioning, balance, and full-body movement.

Good for: Anyone who wants to learn boxing or improve fitness. Beginners should learn technique before increasing intensity.

Example session: Warm-up, footwork drills, shadowboxing rounds, bag practice, and cool-down.

Flexibility

Move comfortably and loosen up a stiff body with stretching and mobility exercises.

About: Gentle stretches and mobility drills that help your joints move comfortably.

Benefits: Supports mobility, posture, and comfort in daily activities and exercise, especially after a long day of work or standing.

Good for: All levels. Move slowly, breathe normally, and never force a stretch or push into pain.

Example session: Gentle warm-up, hip and shoulder mobility, hamstring and calf stretches, then relaxed breathing.
